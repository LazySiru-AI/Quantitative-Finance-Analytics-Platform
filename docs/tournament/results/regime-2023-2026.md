# Market regime, 2023-08-21 → 2026-08-18

**This window is one continuous bull market: SPY total-returned +81.32% over three years, spent
91.6% of its sessions above its own 200-day moving average, and never once failed to recover a
drawdown before the window ended.** Every strategy result measured against this window — including
the tournament in `RESULTS.md` — was tested against a market that mostly went up. That is not a
flaw in the tournament; it is the fact a reader needs before trusting any return number from it.

All numbers below are computed directly from this repo's stored daily bars
(`data/store/bars/interval=1d/`), read via `app.db.duck.read_bars`. Full detail (all symbols, all
drawdown episodes, the full correlation matrix, the full 43-name return list) is in the script
output reproduced at the end of this document; only the material findings are tabulated here.

**Method, stated once:** total return is simple return on `adj_close` (split- and
dividend-adjusted, i.e. a total-return proxy), `last/first - 1`. Annualised volatility is the
sample standard deviation of daily log returns (`ln(P_t/P_{t-1})`) times `sqrt(252)`. Maximum
drawdown is the minimum of `P_t / running_max(P) - 1` over the series, using `adj_close`, reported
with the peak and trough dates that produced it. The 200-day moving average is a simple mean of
`close` (not adjusted), computed from bars starting 2022-09-01 so the window's first session
already has a full 200-session lookback.

half months to fully recover.

## 3. Regime segmentation (SPY 200-day moving average)

Of 751 sessions in the window, SPY closed above its 200dma in **91.61%** of them (688 of 751).
Runs of ten or more *consecutive* below-200dma sessions:

| start | end | length (sessions) | SPY return over run |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025-03-26 | 2025-05-09 | 32 | −0.75% |
| 2026-03-19 | 2026-04-07 | 13 | −0.09% |

Both runs correspond to the tail ends of drawdown episodes in section 2 (the Feb–Apr 2025 and
Jan–Mar 2026 selloffs). Both are short — 32 and 13 sessions — and SPY was roughly flat, not
falling, across each run: by the time the index had spent ten straight sessions below its 200dma,
most of the damage in each episode had already been done and the index chopped rather than
continued to sell off.

SPY's blind-period return computed here (+12.99%) is consistent with the benchmark
`RESULTS.md` reports for the same 127 sessions traded through the mock broker
(+12.84%) — the small difference is expected, since that figure includes broker fills, fees and
slippage and this one is raw index total return. The blind period was not calmer than the
in-sample period on a volatility basis for either symbol: SOXX's annualised vol was *higher* in
the 127-session blind window (55.09%) than across the prior three years (35.50%), and it carried a
deeper drawdown (−29.01%) than all but the largest in-sample episode. SPY's blind-period max
drawdown (−8.58%) was milder than its largest in-sample one, but not negligible.

## What this window does not contain

- **No sustained bear market.** The deepest SPY drawdown was −18.76%, short of the conventional
  −20% threshold, and it fully recovered in 88 sessions. SPY spent 91.6% of all sessions above its
  200dma; the longest stretch below it was 32 consecutive sessions (~6.5 weeks).
- **No down calendar year.** Every full-year and partial-year segment measured for SPY was
  positive: +9.01% (partial 2023), +25.59% (2024), +18.01% (2025), +12.93% (partial 2026).
- **No bond-market rout.** `TLT` (long Treasuries) round-tripped to essentially flat (+0.13%) over
  three years rather than suffering a sustained repricing shock; `AGG`'s worst drawdown was only
  −4.82%.
- **No drawdown that failed to recover.** All six SPY drawdown episodes deeper than 5% recovered
  within the window — none is a "not recovered, window ended first" case.
- **A regime dominated by one theme.** The best three individual performers in the 43-symbol
  roster (`LITE` +1695%, `CRDO` +1556%, `MU` +1387%) and the top of the semiconductor complex are
  all AI-infrastructure names; the mean pairwise correlation among the seven semiconductor names
  (0.582) shows this was not seven independent bets.

None of this is a forecast. It is a description of what three years of stored daily bars actually
contain, offered so that a strategy's return over this exact window can be read next to the regime
that produced it rather than in isolation.
